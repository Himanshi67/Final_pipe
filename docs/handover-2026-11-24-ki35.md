## 2. Decisions (this session)
- **Series-word reject only when the SKU states a litre size.** WHY: the sweep showed the
  unconditional reject losing likely-correct pages ("USHA Umang" sewing machine, "Foxsky 32"
  TV). A false reject costs a NOT FOUND; a weak hit only costs a manual check.
- **One matching series word is enough.** WHY: colour/filler words in a SKU must not reject
  a correct page. Stop words: with, new, and, for, the, plus, white, black, blue, grey,
  gray, silver, red, ivory, gold. "pro" and "instant" stay series words.
- **Weak hits rank by `(loose_overlap, rec_score)`.** `loose_overlap` is 0 when a model code
  exists, so model-coded picks do not change.
- **GWH = `["Water Heaters", "Geysers"]`** in `[sku.input_codes]`; it only gives a category
  hint when the SKU STARTS with GWH.
- **KI-38 recorded, not fixed.** WHY: pre-existing `sku_parts` behaviour, outside KI-35.
- **BOM in the subject of `80c1322`: left as is.** WHY: removing it needs a force-push.
- **KI-39 missing size: stays `weak`, not rejected** (agent decision; owner may override).
  WHY: matches the electric path; every weak pick is checked by hand.
